Duplicates panel

The repository's groups of byte-identical files, worst reclaimable space first. A group is a set of files sharing a content hash; its reclaimable size is what deleting all but one copy would free.

Finding duplicates

Groups do not appear on their own: run mf:duplicate scan (the Scan button, or the command) and the scan reports through the task bar like reconcile does. It is the GUI half of mf duplicate scan, and it runs the ordinary whole-repository scan — its options stay on the CLI. Cancelling keeps what it had already found.

The panel

Groups are listed collapsed, showing the reclaimable size, the group size, how many copies there are, and the hash. Navigate with ↑ or k / ↓ or j, Home / End, and:

  • expand (Enter) — open a group into its members' paths. A + marks a member that shares an inode with another, so it is already a hard link and costs nothing extra.
  • find (f) — jump to a group by hash or to a copy by path (see find).
  • r — reload the groups.

Moving the cursor publishes the row as the selection, so the detail panel and the shared file actions follow it — on a member row the file's metarecord, on a header row the group's own metarecord (hash, size, counters are its fields).

Deleting copies

Which copy survives is always your choice: nothing here picks one for you, and no action spans groups. Both deletions act on the row under the cursor and both are confirmed.

  • keep this one (Shift+k, and the first right-click item) — trash the group's other copies. The confirmation names every path it will trash.
  • trash this one (Delete) — that copy alone, saying what the group is left with.

Deleted copies go to the trash, so a mistake is recoverable. To see what else is identical to a metarecord from anywhere else, metarecord-list:query duplicates sets the list's query to same(mfr_duplicate_group, …) — an ordinary, editable query.
