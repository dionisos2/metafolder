* Storage: what is left after the dense id work
The invariant "a record keeps its dense id between two reindexes" is built (doc
"Deleting a metarecord in the store"). What is left:

- =scripts/dev/bench.sh --quick= (16 November 2026) shows =query.subtree_range_text=
  at about 6.3 ms against a recorded 0.56 ms. It is not the dense id work (the same
  at 786dce1e, before it): it comes from the postings changes (derived 11 to 14),
  not yet bisected. The history was not appended with that run.
- Open questions, none decided (doc "Storage open questions"): no =Nothing= in a
  mass add or remove, replace and rename not being mass writes, the marks a deleted
  record loses, compressing what the log keeps of a deletion.
- The =undo_redo_properties= suite takes about 3 minutes on four cores; the cost
  left is the writes of its cases, not their set-up.
- Left by the review of 17 November 2026 (=notes-performance-stockage.txt=, point 5
  of the REPRISE): =tests/kv_fold_schedule.rs= does not draw positions (folders,
  =tree_ref=) yet; =nom-exif= (embedded metadata) still pulls in =chrono=, declared
  unmaintained (RUSTSEC-2026-0543) — no version without it, to replace or do without;
  =roaring= 0.12, =phf_shared= 0.14 and =schemars= 1 are a major version ahead.
