* A record keeps its dense id between two reindexes
Decided with the user (17 November 2026), in this order, test first each time.
Background: the undo/redo property tests (=crates/daemon/tests/undo_redo_properties.rs=)
found that a record deleted one by one and given back got a new dense id
(=derive_created=, =KvTxn::new_id=) while the stubs of the mass writes, the
postings and the pending entries name records by dense id. Two things were done
meanwhile: =mass_revert_log= refuses ids that are not in the universe (so such a
revert goes one record at a time), and a deletion given back keeps the rows it took
(marks under =THAWED_PART=, doc "Mass operations in the log"). The invariant below
removes the cause. The ids and uuids tables only grow between reindexes already;
only =derive_created= hands out a new id for a known uuid.

Done (steps 1 to 4, one commit): the tests of the invariant, and the restoration —
=Writer::restore_metarecord=, =KvTxn::restore_deleted=, used by the revert of a
deletion and by a creation under a known uuid (=restore_known=). A deletion keeps the
record (and the fold of a mass deletion too) until a reindex, as planned: the
restoration reads it, not the log (flag =kept=, derived 15). A deletion of many records
pending is given back for one operation by =mass_undelete_log=. The slow path of a
folder deleted with live children is gone (=check_forest_integrity= refuses it at
the commit). =move_target= and the new id for a known uuid are gone.

Step 5 done too: =mass_log= filters its ids by the universe, so that no write by ids
names a deleted record (a write by uuids already did, through =dense_ids_of=).

Already true and relied on: a reindex begins with a full fold (=fold_all_mass=),
freezes the marks of records given back (=freeze_all_given_back=) and turns every
stub's ids into uuids in the order of the ids (=stubs_to_uuids=); a deletion of
many records is one stub, and a restoration of sixteen or more of its operations is
done in one pass (=undelete_pending=).

Also noted (doc "Storage open questions"): no =Nothing= in a mass add or remove,
replace and rename not being mass writes, the marks a deleted record loses,
compressing what the log keeps of a deletion. And the =undo_redo_properties= suite
takes about 9 minutes: it should be brought under 2 without losing the cases that
found the bug (fewer fold masks for the larger size, a subset of the pairs).
Not measured yet: =scripts/dev/bench.sh --quick= after the postings change (every
value a posting, derived 11 to 14).
